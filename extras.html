Amiibo - Air Ride All Tracks created by noodl999

Rules
  • This run is done in the Race mode in the Air Ride menu.
  • Your amiibo must be the only rider in this run including other amiibo and human players. You can do this by selecting "P1" on the bottom right of the character/machine select screen and setting your amiibo to P1. One other CPU is allowed, due to there being no way to disable them completely. The CPU must be set to a level 1 Kirby on a Warp Star with the maximum Handicap level.
  • You must use the same amiibo throughout the entire run, but your amiibo can switch machines during the run.
  • In-game rules must be set to the below settings. Rules that aren't mentioned are up to user discretion. Your video recording must show your rules in order for your run to qualify.
    • Game Tempo: Normal
    • No. of Riders: 2
    • Rules: Laps
    • No. of Laps: Default
    • Boost Assist: Off
    • Special Assist: Off
    • Enemies: On
  • This is an IGT category, meaning you must sum up your amiibo’s times for each course at the end of your run.
  • Courses can be played in any order, but your amiibo may not play any course more than once in a single run. Try not to stall during course selection.
  • No glitches/clips are allowed on any courses. If your amiibo performs one accidentally, your run is forfeit and you must start it over.
  • Partial video recordings will not be accepted. In game audio must be audible. Capture card recordings are preferred, but camera recordings of a screen may be accepted on a case by case basis.

Online Checklist - 85 Checkboxes created by Kobazco

Rules
  • Prior to starting starting, delete only your Online checklist. You can have all riders and machines unlocked.
  • Paddocks only.
  • Other players can not join your paddock, and you can not join someone else's paddock (everything must be done solo, vs CPUs)
  • Time starts on selecting Create Paddock option in the connect submenu.
  • Time stops when your 85th checkbox is unlocked on the checklist screen.
  • Partial video recordings will not be accepted. In game audio must be audible. Capture card recordings are preferred, but camera recordings of a screen may be accepted on a case by case basis.

City Trial - Max Stats

Rules
  • This run is done in the Trial mode in the City Trial menu. Online runs will not be accepted.
  • Time starts the moment the City Trial match starts, and ends the frame the last stat is collected.
  • This is an IGT category. Please subtract the remaining time displayed in-game the frame the last stat is collected from the total duration of the match On YouTube, you can move frame by frame with the , and . keys.
  • The in‑game rules, including but not limited to CPUs, match time, and events, are up to player discretion.
  • Any amount of controllers is allowed, as long as only one player is controlling all of them.
  • Partial video recordings will not be accepted. In‑game audio must be audible. Capture card recordings are preferred, but camera recordings of a screen may be accepted on a case by case basis.
